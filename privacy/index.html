SwitchSnitch Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

SwitchSnitch is a network diagnostic app for Android, published on Google Play by WhiteUmbr3lla ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: SwitchSnitch does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or device data.

No internet access

SwitchSnitch does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network. Everything it shows is processed on your phone.

What the app processes on your device

  • Network announcements from your switch. When you connect a USB Ethernet adapter, the app reads the LLDP and CDP announcements that switches send (for example switch name, port, VLAN, management IP address) and shows them on screen.
  • Recent connections. The app keeps your last 10 connections in its private storage on your phone so you can look them back up. You can delete them at any time with the clear button, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Diagnostic log. The app keeps a short technical log (the last 300 lines) in memory only. It is deleted when the app closes.
  • Light sensor. If you choose the "Auto" appearance, the app reads the ambient light level to switch between light and dark themes. The reading is used on the spot and not stored.
  • Capture files. If you open a .pcap file, the app reads only the file you pick, on your device.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it. If you use "Share", "Copy" or "Share diagnostic log", Android's share menu sends the text to the app you pick (for example your email app). The diagnostic log contains your phone model, Android version, connected USB adapters and recent app log lines, which can include switch names and IP addresses. You can read and edit it before sending.

If you send a diagnostic log to our support address, we use it only to help with your issue and delete it once the issue is resolved. We never sell or share it.

Third parties

SwitchSnitch contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Google Play may collect crash reports and usage statistics about apps under Google's own privacy policy, if you have allowed that on your device.

Children

SwitchSnitch is a tool for network technicians and is not directed at children.
